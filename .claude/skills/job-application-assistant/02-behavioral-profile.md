# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Dylan Michael's self-described working style is a **high-pace Operator / Builder** pattern. He thrives in fast-paced environments, is comfortable with long hours, and prefers owning delivery end-to-end (campaigns, sites, automations) rather than slow, highly process-heavy cultures.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Pace / urgency | High | Energized by speed, volume, and shipping under pressure |
| Ownership | High | Prefers clear accountability for client and campaign outcomes |
| Hands-on building | High | Likes implementing SEO, ads, websites, and AI workflows himself |
| Structure / bureaucracy | Lower | Less energized by heavy process, slow approvals, or pure theory roles |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **High output under pressure:** Comfortable with long hours and demanding client timelines
- **Practical problem solving:** Turns marketing and ops friction into workflows, SOPs, and automations
- **Client-facing ownership:** Combines delivery with relationship management (agency + service background)

## How You Work Best
- Fast-paced teams with clear ownership and visible results (traffic, leads, conversions, time saved)
- Roles that mix marketing execution with AI/automation building
- Environments where initiative and stamina are rewarded over pedigree alone

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Formal credentials:** Highest listed education is high school — frame strengths via portfolio, certifications (Google Ads, Claude), and measurable client outcomes
- **Deep ML/engineering roles:** Not a research or software-engineering profile — position as applied AI for marketing/ops, not model training or backend systems

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- Fast-paced, high-growth, ownership, hustle, wear many hats, client-facing, results-driven, self-starter
- Hands-on, build and ship, automation, practical AI, agency environment

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- Slow, highly bureaucratic process; research-only; PhD/degree-required academic culture; 9-to-5 strict boundaries with no intensity
- Pure software engineering / ML research without marketing application

## Management Style Preferences
- Managers who give ownership, clear goals, and room to execute quickly
- Less effective under micromanagement or endless approval chains that block shipping

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Emphasize pace, ownership, and concrete delivery (SEO lifts, campaigns, Claude workflows) — energetic but not arrogant
- **CV:** Lead with measurable marketing outcomes and applied AI automation; avoid claiming software-engineering depth you do not have
- **Interviews:** Use Phantom Avenue (+30% traffic) and Lumin multi-account SEO/AI delivery as STAR anchors; be ready to explain AI workflows without inventing hour-savings metrics until quantified
- **Don't overstate:** Do not invent quantified "hours saved" figures, degrees, or engineering credentials
